Project 1: Menu Variations

This project is a digital menu designed to simulate a local restaurant of my choice, The Clover and the Bee. I initially experimented with Markdown and HTML to try out different ways to present information and group them, and communicate the ambience of the website through modifying elements such as type and color. My final website creates hierarchy through a clean grid-based design and variation in typography, and a key feature is the ability to switch between different modes of brunch and wine bar, allowing visitors to interact with the site while visually experiencing the shift in atmosphere between daytime and evening dining. Every menu and info about the restaurant is purposefully laid out to prevent any overload and maintain structure.
