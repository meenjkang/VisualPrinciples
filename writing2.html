What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you?


A human-made creation that consistently brings out the best in me is the Korean monochrome painting movement, known as Dansaekhwa. Artists such as Park Seo-bo and Ha Chong-Hyun deeply inspire me, with the seemingly restrained surfaces but a deeply generative process behind. Dansaekhwa is influenced by Zen Buddhism as the movement emphasizes repetition of process, emptiness, recognition of the self, and dissolution of ego in the act of making. The sense of immediacy and rawness that is captured in these works gives way to a process that unfolds gradually over time, as the artist allows the material itself to shape the final image. When viewing Dansaekhwa art, I feel like I am forced to become attentive to the subtle elements without distractions, in which my mind and body are fully aligned with the act of the hand. In that moment I think my internal senses are connected to an external system almost like meditating on the painting, and places me into a state of concentrated stillness. Whenever I feel overwhelmed with whatever I am struggling with, I like to visit the National Museum of Contemporary Art in Seoul, which has a great collection of Korean monochrome paintings, and just sit in front of the artworks for hours.
