When do you cherish the slow or hard way of doing something?


I genuinely cherish the slow, even tedious process of cleaning. Whenever I feel overwhelmed or overstimulated, I find myself drawn to deep cleaning my surroundings for hours. On a daily basis, I might quickly dust or vacuum my room, but when I take the time to clean thoroughly, I move more deliberately to wipe down every surface, dust my books, and go through the small nooks and corners of my room. I pay attention to areas I would normally overlook and sometimes even rearrange the furniture, using the process to reset my mind. In doing so, I often come across things I didn’t realize I had lost, as if the act of slowing down allows me to see my familiar space differently. Because it takes time and effort, the result feels more earned, and I become more aware of the space I inhabit.
