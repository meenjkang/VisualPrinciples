The usage of AI is prevalent across all forms of media, from images to writings. This is deceptive to the reader because as Ullendorff mentions about how she screens job applications or several accusations towards media publications, the viewer has to keep questioning whether the thing they are seeing is generated by AI or not, and same for images seen on social media. As such, Ullendorff added a self-made Turing Test in the rubric to distinguish her student’s works from AI; a Turing Test is a test that determines how much the machine’s behavior is indistinguishable from human work. Her test questions if the work is from a human perspective, has a clear target audience, and belongs in a particular context. I think this method would really work well because it gives a clear guideline for students to think about their work and not rely on machine-learnt information.
