The documentary visualizes how Brian Eno works as a sound designer. It was interesting how the algorithmic function of the film being generative reflected Eno’s philosophy, where his music also provides layers of spatial experience generated differently each time, and how he refers to the soundscape as a form of geography and creatures that really texturize the sounds and the way Eno hears the world. As an artist myself I deeply resonated with Eno’s struggles between synthesizing the child and the critic inside me as mentioned in the film when making art, a tension between unlimited play and rigorous skills. Eno reflects on his own process by admitting that he once prioritized technological knowledge as the way of valuing his work, but as he realizes that feelings are the beginning of thoughts, the techonlogical aspects are reduced into mere guidance. By doing so this allowed him to work with more simplicity and in the realm of emotion. Ultimately, Eno suggests the significance of such art in connecting people and their emotions.
