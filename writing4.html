What is something you appreciate or long for from the past?


What I find myself longing for is the contained and predictable feeling of my small childhood town in Seoul. At the time, I always took the same route to school, met the same people, stopped by the same stores, and passed the same familiar views. Not only the places I visited but also what I was allowed to do were limited. I would stop by Yangjaecheon on my way home, crossing the stepping stones, and with an allowance of about 3 dollars, I would end up buying the same ice cream each time at the convenience store. My world was small and I knew it completely. Everything operated in a same routine, and in fact I didn’t even feel the need to look beyond it. That certainty of knowing what each day would look like and where I belonged made my world feel peaceful and safe in a bubble. Now as I’ve grown older, that structure has largely disappeared. I have far more freedom in how I spend my time and where I go, but that freedom comes with a different kind of pressure: the need to constantly make decisions and take responsibility for their outcomes. Instead of moving within a bounded system, I’m expected to make one for myself. At times, that feels disorienting, and sometimes I even find myself wishing for someone to tell me what to do like the old times.
