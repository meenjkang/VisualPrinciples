The interview discusses how a good website can be determined. As an example, the diner’s website is brought up and through this example the panels point out that a good website should convey the atmosphere of the physical space or object through text and images, even without particular photographs of the subject. The dimension of the website also adds to the user experience to present the subject more tactile and expressive when translated onto a flat web space. I agreed on their point that those websites that just exist as little more than a formality should be modified to use easier tools for flexibility of updates regardless of that person having experience of coding or not.
