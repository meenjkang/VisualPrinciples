Laurel discusses the fundamental structure of a website and its need in modern society, emphasizing its flexibility to modify- like a house, the potential to grow itself, and the capability of customized storing. The power of interconnection of the web makes it possible for users to not only create content but also the structure of the environment in which the objects can exist. I agree that for a creative person, personal websites can definitely complement one’s works to strengthen their personal world. What most resonated with me was where Laurel mentions the World Wide Web of the internet in reality is the conversation between different individual computers, and due to its individuality, each website contributes to our responsibility when using the endless possibilities of the web and making intentional authorship.
